# RimHealth Serums

Eight mechanite serums for RimWorld that fix the damage between a bandage and a healer mech serum. They cover a lost nose or finger, scarred organs, addictions, illnesses of old age, harmful mechanites, slow immunity and age. A doctor gives them through the Operations list. Exotic goods traders sell them and crafting can be switched on in the settings.

## Requirements

- RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7

## Building

Run `Source/Build.ps1`. It builds the 1.7 and 1.6 assemblies and copies the mod files to `Mods/RimHealthSerums`.
